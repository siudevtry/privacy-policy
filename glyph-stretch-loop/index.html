Privacy Policy for Glyph Stretch Loop

Last updated: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you install, access, or use the Glyph Stretch Loop browser extension.

Glyph Stretch Loop is a lightweight browser extension designed to animate typed glyphs and text in a seamless horizontal loop. The extension allows users to experiment with visual effects such as wave distortion, adjustable amplitude, motion behavior, and other animation parameters through a convenient toolbar popup interface.

We respect user privacy and design Glyph Stretch Loop to operate with minimal access to user information. The extension is intended to perform its primary functionality directly within the user's browser without requiring personal information to be submitted to the developer.

Privacy Summary

Glyph Stretch Loop does not require an account, does not ask users to submit personal information, does not sell user information, and is not designed to track users across websites.

Text and animation settings entered into the extension are used only to provide the requested text animation and customization functionality.

1. Information We Collect

Glyph Stretch Loop is not designed to collect personally identifiable information from users.

You can use the extension without providing information such as:

  • Your full name;
  • Email address;
  • Phone number;
  • Home or mailing address;
  • Payment or financial information;
  • Government identification information;
  • Account credentials;
  • Demographic information; or
  • Other information intended to directly identify you.

Glyph Stretch Loop does not require users to register for an account, create a user profile, or sign in to a remote service in order to use its core animation functionality.

2. Text Entered Into the Extension

Glyph Stretch Loop allows users to enter text or glyphs that can be used as the visual content of an animation.

The text you enter is used for the purpose of displaying, previewing, and animating that text according to your selected settings.

The extension is not intended to use typed text for advertising, profiling, behavioral analysis, marketing, or unrelated purposes.

Users should avoid entering sensitive information into the extension when such information is not necessary for its intended functionality.

3. Animation and Customization Settings

Glyph Stretch Loop may process configuration values selected or entered by the user in order to generate the requested animation.

These settings may include information such as:

  • Animation amplitude;
  • Wave distortion values;
  • Movement or loop behavior;
  • Animation speed;
  • Text-related visual settings;
  • Display preferences;
  • Effect configuration; and
  • Other values required to render the animation.

These values are functional settings rather than personally identifiable information. They are used to provide the features requested by the user.

4. Local Processing

Glyph Stretch Loop is designed so that its primary text animation and customization functionality can be performed within the user's browser.

Local processing helps minimize unnecessary transmission of user-provided information to external systems.

When users enter text, change animation parameters, adjust amplitude, or modify wave distortion settings, those values are used by the extension to generate the corresponding visual output.

The extension does not require text entered for animation purposes to be submitted to a remote account in order to provide its primary functionality.

5. Browser Storage

Depending on the version and configuration of Glyph Stretch Loop, the extension may use browser-provided storage mechanisms to preserve functional preferences or settings.

For example, browser storage may be used to remember values such as your most recently selected animation settings so that the extension can provide a more convenient experience the next time it is opened.

Information stored for this purpose is used only to support extension functionality and user preferences.

Browser storage used by the extension is not intended to create advertising profiles or track users across unrelated websites.

Users may generally remove locally stored extension information by clearing relevant browser data or uninstalling the extension, depending on the browser being used.

6. Browsing History and Website Activity

Glyph Stretch Loop is not designed to collect or maintain a history of the websites you visit.

The extension does not use browsing history to build advertising profiles, determine user interests, or track browsing behavior across websites.

The extension is also not intended to collect:

  • Your general browsing history;
  • Your search engine history;
  • A list of websites you have previously visited;
  • Your online shopping history;
  • Your social networking activity; or
  • Your activity across unrelated websites for advertising purposes.

7. Website Content

Glyph Stretch Loop is designed specifically to provide its stated text animation functionality.

It is not intended to collect, archive, or analyze the general content of webpages that users browse.

Any interaction with webpage content, if required by a particular feature of the extension, should be limited to what is technically necessary to provide that feature.

Website content is not used by Glyph Stretch Loop to create user advertising profiles or to sell information about users.

8. Account Information

Glyph Stretch Loop does not require users to create a dedicated Glyph Stretch Loop account.

Accordingly, the extension does not maintain a user account database containing usernames and passwords for its standard functionality.

Users should never provide passwords, authentication credentials, credit card numbers, banking information, or other highly sensitive information as text to be animated by the extension.

9. Financial and Payment Information

Glyph Stretch Loop does not process payments as part of its core functionality and is not designed to collect financial information such as bank account numbers, payment card numbers, transaction credentials, or financial authentication information.

10. Location Information

Glyph Stretch Loop does not require precise geographic location information in order to animate text or provide wave distortion effects.

The extension is not designed to track users' physical movements or create location histories.

11. Health and Sensitive Personal Information

Glyph Stretch Loop does not require health information, biometric information, government identification numbers, political information, religious information, sexual orientation, or other sensitive personal information for its intended functionality.

Users should not intentionally enter sensitive personal information into the extension unless they understand that the text will be displayed as part of the animation they create.

12. Cookies

Glyph Stretch Loop does not rely on advertising cookies to provide its core functionality.

The extension is not intended to place cookies on unrelated websites for the purpose of tracking users, retargeting advertisements, or monitoring browsing behavior.

Websites independently visited by users may use their own cookies. Those cookies are governed by the privacy policies of the respective websites and are outside the control of Glyph Stretch Loop.

13. Analytics

Glyph Stretch Loop does not use user-entered text for advertising analytics or behavioral profiling.

If anonymous technical diagnostics or aggregate usage measurements are introduced in a future release, this Privacy Policy may be updated to describe what information is processed, why it is processed, and which service providers are involved.

Such changes will not alter the purpose of previously collected information without appropriate notice where required.

14. Advertising

Glyph Stretch Loop does not sell user information to advertising companies.

The extension does not use user-entered text, animation settings, or browsing activity for targeted advertising or cross-site behavioral advertising.

We do not create advertising profiles based on the text or effects users create with Glyph Stretch Loop.

15. Sale of Personal Information

We do not sell personal information collected through Glyph Stretch Loop.

We also do not rent user information or exchange user information with third parties in return for advertising or marketing benefits.

16. Sharing of Information

Glyph Stretch Loop is designed to minimize the collection and sharing of user information.

We do not share user-entered text with third parties for purposes such as:

  • Targeted advertising;
  • Marketing campaigns;
  • Lead generation;
  • Data brokerage;
  • User profiling;
  • Cross-site tracking; or
  • Sale of personal information.

Information may only be disclosed where required by applicable law, regulation, court order, or another valid legal process.

17. Third-Party Websites

Users may use their browser to access websites or online services that are operated independently of Glyph Stretch Loop.

This Privacy Policy applies only to the Glyph Stretch Loop extension and does not govern the privacy practices of unrelated websites, platforms, services, or browser providers.

We encourage users to review the privacy policies of third-party websites and services they choose to use.

18. Browser Permissions

Browser extensions may require certain permissions in order to provide their functionality.

Glyph Stretch Loop should request only permissions that are reasonably related to the operation of its features.

Permissions are not intended to be used to collect unrelated personal information, monitor unrelated browsing behavior, or build user profiles.

If new functionality requires additional browser permissions in a future version, those permissions may be disclosed through the browser's extension installation or update interface.

19. Data Retention

Because Glyph Stretch Loop is not designed to maintain a remote database of user-entered text or personal information, we do not intentionally retain such information on external servers for the standard operation of the extension.

Functional settings stored locally by the browser may remain on the user's device until they are overwritten, cleared, or removed through browser settings or extension removal.

20. Data Security

We aim to design Glyph Stretch Loop using data-minimization principles, meaning the extension should process only the information reasonably necessary to provide its features.

Limiting unnecessary collection and transmission of information reduces the amount of user data exposed to potential security risks.

However, no software environment or electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their own browser, operating system, device, and installed software.

21. Children's Privacy

Glyph Stretch Loop is a general-purpose creative browser tool and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

The extension does not knowingly request personal information from children as a condition of using its core features.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected in a manner inconsistent with this Privacy Policy or applicable law, we will take reasonable steps to address the situation.

22. International Users

Glyph Stretch Loop may be available to users in different countries and regions.

Because the extension's main functionality is intended to operate locally within the browser without requiring the submission of personal information to a central user database, the amount of personal information processed by the developer is limited.

Users remain responsible for using the extension in accordance with laws and regulations applicable in their jurisdiction.

23. Your Privacy Choices

Users maintain control over whether they install and use Glyph Stretch Loop.

You may stop using the extension at any time. You may also uninstall it through your browser's extension management interface.

Depending on your browser, you may also be able to clear locally stored extension settings and other browser data.

24. Automated Decision-Making

Glyph Stretch Loop does not use user information to make automated decisions that produce legal, financial, employment, insurance, credit, or similarly significant effects concerning individual users.

The extension's automated processing is limited to generating visual animation output based on settings selected by the user.

25. User Profiling

Glyph Stretch Loop does not use entered text, animation preferences, or browsing activity to create behavioral profiles of users.

The extension is not designed to infer personal characteristics, interests, purchasing preferences, political views, health conditions, or other sensitive traits from user activity.

26. Do Not Track

Because Glyph Stretch Loop is not designed to track users across unrelated websites for advertising purposes, it does not use browser activity to create cross-site tracking profiles.

31. Data Minimization
