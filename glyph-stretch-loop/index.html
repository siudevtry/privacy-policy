Privacy Policy for Glyph Stretch Loop

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Glyph Stretch Loop is a browser extension that allows users to animate typed glyphs in a seamless horizontal loop with wave distortion and adjustable amplitude. The extension provides a simple toolbar popup interface for customizing dynamic text effects.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Glyph Stretch Loop.

1. Information We Collect

Glyph Stretch Loop does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

The extension does not require users to create an account or provide personal details such as a name, email address, phone number, physical address, or payment information.

2. Text Entered by Users

Text entered into Glyph Stretch Loop is used only to generate and display the requested animation and visual effects.

User-entered text is processed locally within the browser and is not sent to our servers or shared with third parties.

3. Animation Settings

Settings such as wave distortion, animation amplitude, and other visual configuration options are used only to control the appearance and behavior of the text animation.

These settings are processed within the browser for the purpose of providing the extension's functionality.

4. Browsing Activity

Glyph Stretch Loop does not monitor, collect, or record your browsing history, search history, website activity, or the content of websites you visit.

5. Personal Data

Glyph Stretch Loop does not collect personally identifiable information or sensitive personal information.

We do not use the extension to create user profiles, track users across websites, or identify individual users.

6. Third-Party Services

Glyph Stretch Loop does not sell, rent, or transfer user data to third parties for advertising, analytics, profiling, or marketing purposes.

7. Data Storage

We do not maintain a remote database containing personal information collected through Glyph Stretch Loop.

Any information temporarily required to generate text animations or maintain extension settings is used only for the extension's intended functionality.

8. Data Security

Glyph Stretch Loop is designed to minimize the handling of user data. Text and animation parameters are processed locally whenever required for the extension to operate.

9. Children's Privacy

Glyph Stretch Loop does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the functionality of Glyph Stretch Loop changes or when necessary to meet legal, platform, or regulatory requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Glyph Stretch Loop, please contact the developer using the contact information provided on the extension's official listing or website.
